Flash cards could include a comment section where it allows the user to reflect on what they did know and what they didn't know

## Resolution

Implemented as two optional per-card reflection fields shown after the answer is revealed: **What did you already know?** and **What was unclear or missing?** The notes appear in that open session's recap, stay in memory only while the deck is open, and are cleared when the learner leaves. They are not sent to the backend or saved.
